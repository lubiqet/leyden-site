# LEYDEN — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 September 2026._

## The short version

LEYDEN collects nothing about you and sends nothing off your device. There are no
accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no tracking.

## What we store, and where

- **On your device only:** your Ledger (boards played, streak, personal bests,
  charge-efficiency history) and your settings (sound, haptics, daily reminder).
  These live in the operating system's standard app storage and, if you use it,
  your own platform cloud backup (iCloud). We never receive this data.
- **We operate no server,** so there is nothing for us to collect.

## What we do NOT collect

- No name, email, account, or login.
- No advertising identifier (IDFA), vendor ID, or device fingerprint.
- No location, contacts, photos, or usage analytics.
- No third-party analytics, attribution, ad, or social SDKs.

## Notifications

If — and only if — you turn on the optional daily reminder, the app schedules a
single local notification on your device ("Today's board is live"). It is off by
default, involves no server, and sends us nothing. You can turn it off any time.

## Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, which is the cleanest
position under COPPA and GDPR Article 8.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or erase
on our side. Deleting the app removes the local data; your platform backup is
controlled by you through your device settings.

## Changes and contact

If a future version adds a server feature (e.g. a leaderboard), this policy will
be updated before that feature ships and will describe exactly what changes.

Contact: open a GitHub issue at <https://github.com/lubiqet/leyden-site/issues>.
