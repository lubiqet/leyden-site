# LEYDEN — Support

LEYDEN is a free daily puzzle. Here's how to get help.

## Frequently asked

**How do I play?**
Open the app and tap the ⋯ menu → **How to play** for a short, illustrated
walkthrough. In short: swap adjacent tiles to line up three or more of a colour,
manage your charge, and suppress the discharge to clear the board.

**One puzzle a day — where's more?**
Everyone gets the same daily board. For unlimited play, open the ⋯ menu and choose
**Practice (unranked)**.

**Is my data collected?**
No. LEYDEN collects nothing and sends nothing off your device. See the
[Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

**How do I turn sound, haptics, or the daily reminder on/off?**
⋯ menu → toggles for **Sound**, **Haptics**, and **Daily reminder**. The daily
reminder is off until you turn it on.

**My progress disappeared / I got a new phone.**
Progress is stored on the device. If you restore from an iCloud backup it comes
with you; a fresh install with no restore starts clean.

## Contact

Open a GitHub issue: <https://github.com/lubiqet/leyden-site/issues>

We aim to reply within a few business days. Please include your device model and
iOS version.
