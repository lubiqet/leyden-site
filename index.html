How to play

Learn it in a minute.
Master it over weeks.

Swap adjacent tiles to line up three or more of a colour. Manage your charge. Prime, then discharge to clear the board.

STEP 1

Watch the charge

Every move ticks your clock down. Spend it wisely.

STEP 2

Look ahead

Read the three rows waiting at the top and plan.

STEP 3

Prime a match

Line up and arm groups of the same colour.

STEP 4

Discharge

Release armed groups together — a triple is ×7.

The feel

Six distinct colours, each with its own shape, so the board reads instantly. Matches cascade with a satisfying pop — this little demo runs right here in your browser.

Questions

Good to know

How much does LEYDEN cost?

It's free — no ads, no in-app purchases, and no subscriptions.

Does LEYDEN track me or collect data?

No. LEYDEN collects nothing and sends nothing off your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs. See the privacy policy.

Can I play more than one puzzle a day?

Yes. Everyone gets the same daily board, and there's an unlimited Practice mode when you want more.

Do I need to be online?

No. LEYDEN runs entirely on your device and works fully offline.

Which devices are supported?

LEYDEN is built for iPhone, iOS first.
